# HidroSense logo: usage

The HidroSense logo is the "C10 Telinga tetes" mark: the HidroSense cat with a lime water drop as its right ear. It was approved as a concept on 2026-11-16; the final artwork is waiting for Phase 4 sign-off. Its source is Figma file `KZ7dODi36pWhEPumnRz7RG`, page "Logo v3", sheet "Fase 3 / Konstruksi".

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `hidrosense-mark.svg` | Main logo on a blue tile (in-app, web, documents) |
| `hidrosense-mark-charcoal.svg` | Same on a charcoal tile, for dark backgrounds |
| `hidrosense-mono.svg` | One colour, charcoal, for print, stamps and fax-like output |
| `hidrosense-reversed.svg` | One colour, white, for use on Royal Blue or photos |
| `hidrosense-appicon-ios-blue.svg`, `appicon-ios-blue-1024.png` | iOS app icon master. Full bleed, no corners: iOS applies the mask |
| `hidrosense-appicon-ios-charcoal.svg`, `appicon-ios-charcoal-1024.png` | Alternate dark icon (iOS dark appearance) |
| `hidrosense-appicon-android-foreground.svg` | Android adaptive icon foreground. Background layer: solid `#1565FF` |
| `appicon-ios-blue-512.png`, `appicon-ios-blue-192.png` | Store and web sizes |
| `appicon-48.png` | Hand-tuned 48 px version |

The Figma components are Logo/Mark, Logo/Mark Arang, Logo/Mono, Logo/Reversed, Logo/Size 16, 24, 32, 48, Logo/AppIcon iOS Biru, iOS Arang and Android Foreground.

## Colours

| Part | Hex |
|---|---|
| Tile | Royal Blue `#1565FF` (or Charcoal `#111827`) |
| Cat | Warm white `#FFFDF8` |
| Drop | Electric Lime `#A3F533` |
| Eyes and smile | Charcoal `#111827` |

Contrast: the cat on blue is 4.8:1 and on charcoal 17.5:1. The drop on blue is 3.6:1. Colour-vision separation is ΔE 30 or more for every pair.

## Rules

- **Corner radius:** 22.5% of the tile width (240: 54, 80: 18, 48: 11). A fixed radius on a small tile turns it into a circle.
- **Clear space:** at least the drop's width (one fifth of the tile) on every side.
- **Minimum size:** 16 px. Below 64 px use the Logo/Size versions, which have bigger, wider-set eyes, no smile and a wider drop gap.
- **Android:** keep the whole mark inside the central 66% safe circle.

## Do not

- Recolour the cat lime, or make the drop any colour other than lime (or white or charcoal in the mono versions).
- Put the blue tile on a blue background without the charcoal variant or a clear margin.
- Add outlines (especially around the eyes), shadows, gradients, glow or blur.
- Stretch, rotate or redraw the mark, or remove the gap between the drop and the head.
- Use the lime drop version on white or ice backgrounds without the tile: lime on white is 1.3:1 and the drop disappears.
